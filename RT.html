Rules applied
Exactly what this module does, in order.
1 · Integrity review — flags missing health number, version code, date of birth, gender, provider number and diagnosis.
2 · Non-UHN approver — when the export already carries a referring provider on a brachy row, that admission bills C343A and E082A only, with no X-codes.
3 · Brachy expansion — the technique in the service-code column becomes one line per code:
  GU US HDR / GU US LDR → X313A, X324A, Z611A, Z541A, J138C, C343A, E082A
  GU MR HDR → X313A, X324A, Z611A, Z541A, C343A, E082A
  GYN IS HDR → X313A, X324A, J162C, C343A, E082A
  GYN IVA → X312A, X323A, A343A, then X334A and A348A on the next two days
4 · Admission codes — C343A and E082A switch the location from HOP to HIP, master number 3910, admission date set to the service date.
5 · Referring gap — J138C and J162C are held until a referring provider is entered.
6 · XNone → X312A.
7 · H&N brachy — left exactly as found, for manual coding.
8 · Metastatic / palliative — kept in the main file and also offered as a separate subset for the 512 submission.
