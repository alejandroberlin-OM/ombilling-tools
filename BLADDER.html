🩺 Urologists in the MDBCC
Rules applied
1 · Provider — the oncologist's name becomes their billing number.
2 · Referring provider — the urologist's name becomes their number. Consults (A345A) are flagged when no urologist is named.
3 · Dates — read as day/month/year, as the clinic's own headings say, and written as MM-DD-YYYY. Real date cells are handled too. Anything that could be read either way is flagged rather than guessed.
4 · Diagnostic code — the first three characters, so “188 - Bladder Ca” becomes 188.
5 · Service code — the first four characters plus A, so “A343A - Follow-up” becomes A343A.
6 · Location and master number — always HOP and 4265, whatever the sheet holds.
7 · Blank rows — skipped, so the sheet's unused rows are ignored.

The urologist is read from anywhere in the row, not just the “Urologist in MDBCC” column, because it has been typed into the neighbouring column before now.
