⚙️ Generation
Every row — service or follow-up — is held to one per patient per Monday-to-Sunday week. Where two compete for the same week the real service date is kept and the generated follow-up is dropped. Dropped rows are listed under “Removed duplicates”.
Rules applied
1 · Keep metastatic only — rows whose Intent contains “metastatic”. Primary intent rows are left out entirely.
2 · Service code and quantity — every row becomes G512A with a quantity of 1.
3 · One row per patient per week — weeks run Monday to Sunday inclusive. Where a patient has several services in the same week, the earliest is kept and the rest are listed under “Removed duplicates”.
4 · Follow-up copy — each surviving row is duplicated onto the Monday after its service date. A Monday service moves a full week; a Sunday service moves one day.
5 · One per week, whatever its origin — the weekly rule is then applied again across everything. A service and a follow-up cannot share a week; the real service date wins.
6 · Two output files — rows dated today or earlier go in the submit-now file. Anything later is held in a second file whose name carries the last date it contains, so it is obvious when it can be uploaded.
7 · Integrity — missing health number, version code, date of birth, gender, provider number or diagnosis are flagged before export.

Weeks are keyed by the date of that week's Monday rather than a week number, so a service in late December cannot be confused with one in early January.
