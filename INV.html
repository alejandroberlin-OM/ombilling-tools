What this does
Replaces the three macros in one pass:
1 · Delete pictures, headers and footers — nothing is carried over from the workbook, so the logos and page furniture simply never appear.
2 · Rename worksheets — each sheet is named from the text after “ for ” in cell D2, commas removed, trimmed to 30 characters. Sheets with no such text keep their original name.
3 · Print worksheets to PDF — one landscape letter PDF per sheet, scaled to the page width, with the header row repeated on every page. Filenames replace : / \\ ? * [ ] and spaces with underscores.

The totals come from each sheet's own “Total Fee Submitted” line. Where a sheet has none, the fee column is summed instead and the row is flagged in the review table.
